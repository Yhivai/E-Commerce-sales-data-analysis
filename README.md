📌 E-Commerce Sales Analysis Dashboard

This project involves end-to-end analysis of an e-commerce store's annual sales data to extract meaningful business insights and present them through an interactive dashboard built in Microsoft Excel.

🎯 Objective:
To analyze sales performance across various dimensions — time, geography, demographics, and sales channels — and derive actionable recommendations to boost future revenue.

🛠️ Tools & Techniques Used:
• Microsoft Excel — Pivot Tables, Charts, Slicers
• Data Cleaning & Transformation
• Dashboard Design (Dark Theme)

📊 Key Questions Answered:
• Which month had the highest sales and orders?
• Who buys more — men or women?
• What are the different order statuses and their share?
• Which states contribute the most to revenue?
• How does age group relate to purchasing behavior by gender?
• Which sales channel drives maximum orders?
• What is the highest selling product category?

💡 Key Insights:
• Women account for ~64% of total purchases
• Adults aged 30–49 years contribute ~50% of all orders
• Maharashtra, Karnataka, and Uttar Pradesh are the top 3 revenue-generating states
• Amazon, Flipkart, and Myntra are the dominant sales channels
• March recorded peak sales and order volume
• 92% of orders were successfully delivered

✅ Final Recommendation:
Target women in the 30–49 age group residing in Maharashtra, Karnataka, and Uttar Pradesh through personalized ads, offers, and coupons on Amazon, Flipkart, and Myntra to maximize sales conversion.
